What this review can and cannot tell you

This tool checks the structure of your supplied rule set. It does not fetch URLs, inspect HTTP responses, assess content relevance, execute platform rules, or guarantee search rankings. Paths are case-sensitive; trailing slashes, query strings, and URL origins stay distinct. A full URL and a relative path are different keys. Fragments, credentials, wildcards, whitespace, and regex rules are rejected. Missing inventory entries may be intentionally unchanged or retired: review each one. If conflicting destinations make a chain ambiguous, the tool stops rather than choosing a rule.

No files or project data are uploaded or saved by this application. Reloading clears your work. Your hosting provider may retain ordinary access logs.

Before you move.

Is this a replacement for a crawler?

No. Use your crawler to collect URLs and test the deployed site. MoveLedger reviews the redirect plan and the inventory you provide, before and alongside those checks.

Why pay when free redirect checkers exist?

For basic loop detection, a free checker may be enough. The planned kit combines a larger offline workspace, inventory reconciliation, and reusable handoff documents. Try the free preview before deciding whether that workflow is useful.

Does “no issues found” mean we can launch?

No. It only means the supported structural checks found no issues in your input. Check destination responses, content relevance, canonical tags, indexing rules, internal links, analytics, and rollback readiness separately.

Can this apply redirects to my site?

No. It produces review evidence, not server configuration. Your developer applies reviewed rules using your platform and tests the results.
