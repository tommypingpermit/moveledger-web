Updated September 23, 2026. MoveLedger is an early product preview. Sales are not open.

Local processing

The checker reads files only after you select them, or processes text you paste. It runs in your browser and does not upload file contents, project names, or findings. It does not store them in cookies or local storage. Reloading or closing the tab clears the current workspace. Downloaded reports remain on your device until you remove them.

Website requests

Loading the website makes ordinary requests to its hosting provider, which may log information such as IP address, browser information, requested page, and access time. The application contains no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or external fonts. Following an external link is subject to that destination’s policies.

Scope

The checker inspects supplied exact-match redirect rules. It does not request your URLs, crawl your site, implement redirects, verify SEO outcomes, or act as a launch approval. Reports are editable files, not independently signed attestations. Supported limits and exclusions are shown next to the tool.

Commercial availability

The planned agency edition is a one-time download. Its displayed price is an offer under development; there is currently no checkout, charge, account registration, email collection, or reservation. Seller information, support contact, license terms, refund terms, and payment details must be finalized before sales open.
